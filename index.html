Latest Update
What's New
in v2.1.1
✦
New minimalist design — v2.1.1
A cleaner home screen with live staff and your next services, one search bar for clients, jobs and tools, and a faster menu you can search and filter by Clients, Money or Team.
📆
12-Month Service Interval — v2.1.0
Jobs Done now has a 12 Months option alongside 2, 4, 6 and 8 Weeks and 6 Months — one tap sets the next service date a year after the last service.
💳
Payments Reminder — v2.0.8
Text a client about an outstanding payment straight from the menu. Enter the client name (optional), business, mobile and amount — the chat opens pre-filled, and you can send the invoice PDF into the same chat.
📁
Broadcast Folder — v2.0.8
All message templates now live together in Settings → Broadcast: Payment Reminder, Marketing Template, Request Reviews and Reminder Text.
🗓️
Weekly or Fortnightly — v2.0.7
Daily (Mon–Sun) and Start/Finish Time pay methods now both let you choose Weekly (7 days) or Fortnightly (14 days) right in the calculator — switch any time, your saved pay sheet entries are kept either way.
⏰
Start/Finish Time Pay Method — v2.0.6
New pay calculator method! Enter each employee's clock-in & clock-out time for every day across a 2-week pay period and the app works out hours and pay automatically. Switch between Simple, Daily, or Start/Finish methods any time in Settings.
📤
Send Pay Sheets via WhatsApp — v2.0.6
Send your pay sheet summary — or a single employee's pay — straight to WhatsApp or WA Business with one tap. Names, hours and pay are pre-filled and ready to send.
⭐
One-Tap Review Requests — v2.0.6
When a client's card glows green (service done within the last 7 days), a new "Request Review" button appears. Tap it and your phone's Messages app opens instantly with the client's number, your review message, and the Google review link all pre-filled.
💰
Pay Calculator — v2.0.5
New in this update! Calculate your employees' pay by entering their name, days worked, hours and minutes. Set a default hourly rate (editable anytime for pay rises) and override it per entry. View a full pay sheet summary and export it to PDF in one tap.
📄
Documents Centre
A brand-new Documents section is now available in the Main Menu. Open, fill in, download, or share the Client Service Agreement via WhatsApp — all from within the app.
🔍
Client Name Search
Instantly find any client by name with the new search bar in the Clients tab. Results filter live as you type — no scrolling through long lists.
💎
30-Day Glow Toggle
Under Settings, you can now turn the teal blue glow indicator on or off for clients whose service is due within 30 days. Full control over your view.
⭐
Editable Review Request Message
Customise the Google review SMS sent to clients directly in Settings. The review link is always included automatically — clients get a clean "Click here to review us" link, never a raw URL.
🔒
Secured Google Reviews Link
The Google Reviews link is now hardcoded and hidden inside the app. It can't be viewed or accessed from the UI — and clients always receive a clean hyperlink with 5 stars pre-selected.
👥
Clients & Reminders — One Tab
Clients and Service Reminders are combined under a single tab in the menu, with a smooth sub-tab switcher. Quicker to navigate, easier to use.
📱
Remind Directly from Clients
Tap the "Remind" button on any client card and your phone's native Messages app opens instantly with a pre-filled reminder — no copying, no hassle.
📣
Bulk Prospect SMS
Load your prospects from Google Sheets and send a personalised marketing SMS to every one of them — step by step, with a preview list and progress tracker.
